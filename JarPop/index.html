JarPop

A colorful physics puzzle game for Android. Every piece moves, bounces and settles like a real object.

Coming soon to Google Play

What makes JarPop fun

  • Real physics

    Pieces roll, bounce and settle on their own, so every level is a small puzzle to work out.

  • Bright colors in 2.5D

    A clean, colorful look with depth that’s easy on the eyes.

  • No account needed

    Your progress, coins and settings are saved right on your phone.

  • Free to play

    Play for free with ads, or pick up coin packs, remove ads with a single purchase, or join JarPop Pro.

  • 15 languages

    Play in your own language, from Spanish to Japanese.
